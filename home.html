Timeline Journey

2016

ShowBT Philippines scouts and selects the five members for training.

December 26, 2016

The group performs at "ShowBT Night 2016" as trainees.

May 16, 2018

The final five-member lineup is officially unveiled via social media.

July 2018

The group makes their first national television appearance on TV5’s Aja Aja Tayo!.

October 26, 2018

SB19 officially debuts with the digital single "Tilaluha".

December 19, 2018

The group holds their first mini-concert and fanmeeting, "Start of the Best," in San Juan

July 10, 2019

The group announces their comeback single, "Go Up".

August 16, 2019

SB19 holds their first major press conference at the Luxent Hotel.

2019

SB19 made several TV appearances

October 26, 2019

Launch of the game-show series Sinong Batak for their first anniversary.

November 3, 2019

The fandom name "A'TIN" is officially announced via Facebook Live.

November 20, 2019

SB19 becomes the first Filipino act on the Billboard Next Big Sound chart.

December 25, 2019

The group signs with Sony Music Philippines and releases "Alab (Burning)".

July 31, 2020

Release of debut studio album Get in the Zone.

November 21, 2020

First global online concert, "SB19: Live from Manila".

December 7, 2020

Physical CD release of album and launch of online store.

July 22, 2021

Releases EP Pagsibol.

June 9, 2023

Releases Pagtatag! EP and announces formation of 1Z Entertainment.

June 24–25, 2023

Pagtatag! World Tour kicks off with two sold-out nights.

April 25, 2025

Releases Simula at Wakas EP with MVs for "Dungka!" and "Time".

May 31 – June 1, 2025

SaW World Tour kickoff with historic sold-out shows at Philippine Arena.

April 18, 2026

"Wakas at Simula: The Trilogy Finale" at SMDC Festival Grounds (100,000 attendees).

July 30, 2026

SB19 made their historical performance at Lollapalooza Chicago.

October 6, 2026

SB19 earned three awards at the Filipino Music Awards (FMA) 2026
